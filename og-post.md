Yo OGs, quick add to what I posted. I added the thing about leveling your fighter up:

**🥊 Fight styles (Boxer, Brawler, Street fighter)**
You fight as one of three styles, and it levels up as you win fights (5 levels). Each style levels separately, so you can switch and grind another one.
- **Brawler:** hits harder every level
- **Boxer:** dodging costs less stamina and you recover faster
- **Street fighter:** you get more time to react

Your starting style depends on your character. Tank is a Brawler, Slick is Street, Shade is a Boxer. You can see it and switch it in the **Your Circle** tab under "Your fight style", and it shows on the fight screen too.
