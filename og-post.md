**South Carolina law**
- ⚖️ **Charge classes:** every arrest now has a class, from Class C misdemeanor up to Class A felony. Big distribution is worse than small.
- 💸 **Court costs:** after a conviction you owe a fine plus the 107.5% state surcharge (a $500 fine costs $1,037.50). Pay it in the Money tab. Miss it and you get a bench warrant.
- 🧒 **Juvenile court:** under 18 you pay half with no surcharge, and your parents cover it if you can't (they lose trust). A teen bust means juvenile detention for a few turns.
- 🧑 **Ages 18 to 20:** youthful offender, half the fine.
- 🔎 **Audit heat:** sit on 25K+ dirty cash with no clean money and the heat climbs every day. Launder it.

**Setting and art**
- 📍 The game is set in **Columbia, SC** (blocks, shops and people are made up).
- 🖼️ 4 new pictures for court costs, warrants, audits and detention, plus fixed art on the naloxone, bank flag, burner shop, market, sponsorship, shortage, daily and gig screens.
