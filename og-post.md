📱 **THE HUSTLE v217: START A GROUP CHAT** 📱
Text your people all at once, and your friends finally have a place in Messages.
📲 **Get it first:** PC **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Mac Cmd+Shift+R). iPhone: Safari **aA > Reload Page**; Home Screen app: swipe it closed and reopen (twice if still old). Check it says **v217**. Saves carry over.

**👥 Group chats**
Messages, tap the **✎ pencil**, then **New group chat**. Pick 2 to 6 people, name it, done. Ask who wants to link up, tell them to stay low, or ask for $100. Close friends may send it (once a day). Plugs hate group texts and your trust with them drops. Friends talk to each other in there too.

**💬 Friends are easy to find**
Friends are pinned at the top of Messages, and texting them works again. Your crew chat, **The Gang**, is pinned there too once you have workers.

**📵 Real phone rules**
Dead phone, cut off, taken, or locked up? Group chats lock and tell you why. Cut a friend off and they leave your groups.

**🤝 Plans pay off**
Agree to link up in a group, then actually kick it with that friend the same day for extra closeness.
