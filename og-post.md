🚨 **THE HUSTLE: v210 IS LIVE** 🚨
Thanks for all the bug reports. This one is big.
📲 **Get it first:** PC **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Mac Cmd+Shift+R). iPhone: Safari **aA > Reload Page**; Home Screen app: swipe it closed and reopen (twice if still old). Check it says **v210**. Saves carry over.

**New**
- 🗺️ **A night map for every city** (all 8). Hover a district to see the block. On phones it's block tabs.
- 🏚️ **Full scene screen for every spot** (barber, gym, pawn shop, trap house...). Actions sit on the picture.
- 🎒 **Real loot from licks:** you need a book bag. Pockets hold 4 small things, bag 8 more, TVs and speakers need a car trunk. Jewelry only sells at the pawn shop (40 to 60%). Check the Licks app.
- 🚔 **Cops can stop and search you** while you carry stolen stuff. Talk helps.
- 🔐 **Sort and Pin Cracker mini games** on licks (alarm panels, safes, the pharmacy cabinet, phones). Keyboard works: arrows, numbers, Enter.
- 📞 **Plugs moved to the Move map:** the plug card is off the Block tab. Look for **Plug spots** on Move. 60+ trust = pull up any time. 20 to 59 = text "let me pull up" first and the spot opens for the day. New plugs need "Get introduced". Kick it, fronts, pitching and robbing only show while you are at the meet. They still call you with a one-day discount (40+).
- ⚖️ **Bulk buying:** Low plugs sell grams and single pills (start here), Mid plugs sell ounces and packs of 50, Big plugs sell QPs, pounds and boats. Block tab now shows what you hold and the Js.
- 🧭 **Move map icons:** every spot has a clear icon now instead of a tiny picture.
- 🦹 **Heist crew:** plan a heist and pick a driver, lookout and inside man from your crew. Case the place first (+12% odds), pick the split (Low, Fair, Big), then run it yourself or send the crew (they text you in 2-3 days). Low-loyalty crew can skim or snitch. Licks app has a link to heists.
- 📻 **Police scanner:** cops patrol a few blocks every day (more when you're hot). Serving or hitting licks on a patrolled block brings extra heat. Buy a scanner at the pawn shop ($350, 16+) to see the 🚓 blocks and what's coming (task force sweeps, raid risk, traffic stops) on the Move tab. It can tip you off before a raid.
- 🚕 **Side gigs have mini games now:** Delivery (lane run, 16+), Tow truck (hook timing, 18+) and the new Rideshare driver (18+, your own car). Better runs pay more clean money. Phone > Side hustles.
- 💎 **Drip status:** your chains and fit add up to Fresh, Flashy, Iced out or Untouchable. If your rep can back it, people snitch on you less and crews respect you more. Drip your rep can't carry gets you robbed. Tap your character card to see it.
- 👤 More faces, a loading screen, and 17 logic fixes (age limits, actions only where you are, car moves don't cost the day).
