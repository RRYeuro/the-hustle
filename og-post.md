🚨 **THE HUSTLE: UPDATE v205 IS LIVE** 🚨
📲 **Get it first:** PC **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Mac Cmd+Shift+R). iPhone: Safari **aA > Reload Page**; Home Screen app: swipe it closed and reopen (twice if still old). Check it says **v205**. Saves carry over.

**Living in other cities**
- 📍 The top left now says **Columbia, South Carolina**. Go to the **Airport** (Spots tab, Downtown) and tap **Move** to live in Charlotte, Atlanta, Houston, Miami, New York, Los Angeles or Chicago. Tap **Fly** for a day trip. Need to be 18, no product on you. Fly home any time.
- 🏙️ Every city has its own block names, gangs, plugs and spots. Prices and rent run higher in the big cities.
- 🏠 **Buy a house** in any city, then hit **Rent out house** (Spots, Money or Phone) when you are away. It pays clean cash every week, more in rich cities.
- 🪖 **Street lane:** you cannot start a gang in another city. Fly your soldiers out, recruit locals, take blocks. Every soldier you send makes Columbia weaker. Text your **Right Hand Man** to pick up loads and make drops at home.
- 🏀 **Sports lane:** fly out for away games and matches only. No street work out there.
- 🥂 Miami, New York, LA and Chicago have a **penthouse, exotic car and VIP table**.
- ✈️ No travel cooldown. 🖼️ New faces for every plug, city plug and gang leader. Pictures are smaller and the wide-screen layout has no gaps now.
