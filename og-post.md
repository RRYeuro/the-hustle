**New this drop**
- 🚌 **Moving asks how you want to go:** tap a block and pick Drive ($5, 3 energy), Ride ($25, 18+), Bus (3 energy with a $30 pass, no day lost) or Walk (takes the day). A mis-tap no longer costs your day.
- 🧺 **Trap houses are run in person:** go to your trap block and tap **Run it** (or the 🧺 on the Move map) to open traps, hire workers, give stock, upgrade and fire. Workers text you what they hold and you can text them to bring the money.
- 📱 **Phone grows with you:** it starts with Messages, Contacts, Maps, Family, Wallet, Rides and News. Side hustles and Social show on day 2, Licks day 3, Crew, Orders and Burners at Petty Dealer, Trap house and Backer at Middle Man. New apps get a NEW tag. Full screen on mobile now.
- 💬 **Your connect texts like a person:** chat bubbles in the thread, tap a reply to order or lose the number. The order screen looks like a text now, not a menu.
- 👥 **Your Circle:** crew and family moved out of the phone into one place. The manager button now tells you what's missing.
- 🚩 **Licks show on the map** as red flags. Burners are back at the corner store and the mall. Contacts are A to Z cards.
- 🗣️ **Talk it out says she/her** when you're haggling with a woman plug.
- 🔒 **Locked up means no paycheck.** Jail and juvie don't pay your job anymore. You get paid again when you're out.
- ⏭️ **Skip time button,** street business is back on the Block tab, you stay in your spot after tapping an action, and the big pictures are thumbnails now.
