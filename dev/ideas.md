## Depth (Nov 26)
- One reputation record per person (respect / fear / trust / loyal) that every system reads, so past choices change prices, jobs, texts and story events.
- Heat from repeated patterns (same block, same hour, same plug) instead of random punishment.
- Lifestyle goals (apartment, car, clothes).
- Case file: heat builds evidence from patterns (same corner, big cash deposits, snitches). Lawyer, laundering and laying low shrink it. A raid only happens when the case is strong.
- Books must add up: if a front washes way more than its real sales (bp96 demand vs ledger), auditors notice. High-rep busy businesses can wash more safely.
- Rivals that react: rival crews push into blocks where you are weak (Rivals number), poach Js, undercut prices. Holding a block takes work.
- Supply ripples: a plug raid or drought raises prices city-wide for days, Js switch products, workers sell slower. Stocking up early pays.
- Workers with ambition: underpaid or ignored workers skim, quit, or start their own crew and become rivals. Loyal ones can run a block for you.
- Family needs: partner, parent and kids want time, money and safety. Neglect leads to breakups and worse kid outcomes; showing up pays back.
- City seasons: check week, holidays, summer and winter change demand for products and businesses.
- Clean credit: a clean money history (ledger) builds credit for loans and legit buys; missed payments bring collectors.
