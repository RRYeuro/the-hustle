## Robbery and licks
- Snatching as the very first robbery step (purse/phone snatch on the block, small money, low heat).
- Small guns and pills as loot items from licks.
- Daily lick contracts (someone texts a job with a bonus if you do it today).
- Memory tiles and Rotating locks mini games (keyboard + tutorial card with Start).
- Pawn buyback: pawn your own chain, buy it back within 7 days for price + 20%.

## Connects
- Bring the load home yourself after flying out: stash car (hidden compartment, Car ring can build them), mules (cheap, can snitch), freight (slow, seizure chance), Right Hand drives it, fly with it (fastest, highest bust risk). Checkpoints; Watch network scouts the route; Muscle escorts.
- Kingpin rank opens your own connect line (no talk needed).

## Crew rings
- Right Hand picks up connect loads and stands in for you at meets.
- Ring icons moving on the map while they work.
- Each ring talks in its own voice in The Gang chat (scammers smooth, robbers short, drivers hype); leaders bring up past busts and wins.
- Rings work at their own hours on the clock (car + robbery at night, scam by day, Business Manager during open hours).
- Rings open a branch in another city (send members).

## Houses
- Use a house as a trap (traps are still block-based).
- Neighborhood matters: hood houses = more heat, nice areas = quiet.
- Houses can get raided, robbed or burned in a beef.
