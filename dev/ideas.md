## Depth (Nov 26, from Gemini's notes + my take)
- Direction: depth over features. Make existing systems talk to each other instead of adding new buttons. Target: hard but fair.
- Day engine: each new day rolls up to 5 beats wired to existing systems: 1 personal event, 1 block event, 1 money opportunity, 1 problem, 1 relationship moment. Some are lucky. Reuse skin10 street events + skin46 callbacks, don't duplicate.
- One reputation record per person (respect / fear / trust / loyal) that every system reads, so past choices change prices, jobs, texts and story events.
- Workers: personalities, reliability, skill growth (extend loyal/hustle stats that already exist).
- Heat from repeated patterns (same block, same hour, same plug) instead of random punishment.
- Money choices: reinvest vs save vs spend with real tradeoffs. Never count trading money as income.
- Later: neighborhoods evolve (businesses open/close, rival influence), lifestyle goals (apartment, car, clothes), real music career loop.
