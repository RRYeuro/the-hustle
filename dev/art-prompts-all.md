# Art list (still missing: 2 collages for licks and loot)
Rules: 4-panel 2x2 collage, equal panels, thin black borders, no text, no brands, no logos.

## 1. g_search (cop stop and search) + loot
2x2 collage, four equal panels with thin black borders, gritty realistic street style at night, no text, no brands, no logos. Panel 1: a police cruiser with lights on stopped beside a young man in a hoodie on a sidewalk, officer stepping out. Panel 2: an officer unzipping a plain black book bag on the hood of the cruiser, flashlight beam on it. Panel 3: gold chains, a watch and rings spilled on a pawn shop glass counter under warm light, an older man's hands looking them over. Panel 4: a car trunk open at night with a flat screen TV and speakers loaded inside, alley behind.

## 2. g_keypad (safes, locks and alarm panels)
2x2 collage, four equal panels with thin black borders, gritty realistic style, dark moody lighting, no text, no brands, no logos, blank keypads with no readable numbers. Panel 1: a small steel safe with a glowing keypad inside a bedroom closet, gloved hand reaching for it. Panel 2: a wall alarm panel by a back door with its cover off and colored wires exposed, gloved hands holding wire cutters. Panel 3: a locked glass pharmacy cabinet with a keypad lock behind a counter, shelves of plain pill bottles. Panel 4: a cracked phone lit up in a car seat at night, a thumb over the lock screen.
